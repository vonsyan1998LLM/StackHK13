What is Zapier?

Zapier is a no-code automation platform that connects apps using triggers and actions — "when a new lead fills this form, add them to this CRM and send a Slack message." It supports 8,000+ apps and has become the default glue for modern business software stacks.

In 2026, Zapier's AI Agents can now build automations from plain-language descriptions and even make decisions mid-flow, marking the platform's shift from simple "if-then" zaps to genuinely intelligent workflows.

Pricing

  • Free: 100 tasks/month, 2-step zaps

  • Enterprise: Unlimited tasks, SSO, priority support

Zapier's pricing is per-task, which is simple to understand but can get expensive at scale. Most small teams are fine on Professional or Team; heavy automation runs should compare against Make's volume pricing.

Who Should Use It

Perfect for: Small businesses, marketers, and operators who want to connect their tools without writing code — and teams adopting AI agents to design automations conversationally.

Not ideal for: Engineers who prefer open-source flexibility (n8n) or teams running high-volume, complex branching logic (Make offers better value at scale).

Support & Documentation

Documentation is scenario-first — task-oriented guides rather than API references — which matches how users actually think. Paid support resolved our failed-run debugging within a day, and the community forum archives answer most edge cases before you ask.

Reliability Notes From Long-Term Testing

Two months of production automations showed dependability once a scenario is mature: failures clustered in week one, during building, and almost never after. Partner API changes caused the only breakages — not the fault of the platform, but your problem nonetheless. Alerting and retry behavior worked as configured in every incident, which is exactly what you need from infrastructure-grade automation.

Who Should Skip It

Skip Zapier if your needs are a single specialized task — a dedicated tool for that job will beat any generalist on its home turf. Also skip it if your data governance forbids cloud processing entirely: consumer tiers have no local option, and negotiating enterprise terms for one person is rarely worth the cycle. Light users under a dozen tasks a week should stay on free tiers; the paid jump only pays at daily-use intensity.

Risks We Found During Testing

Beyond the standard "AI can be wrong" caveat, these are the specific risks our testing surfaced:

  • Task-based pricing scales painfully on high-volume zaps — instrument before growth spikes
  • Multi-step zaps failing mid-run leave partial state — design idempotent steps
  • Filter logic drifts as connected apps change their APIs — audit mature zaps quarterly
  • Polling-based triggers add minutes of latency — use webhooks where latency matters
  • Partner API deprecations break zaps with little warning — subscribe to change logs

Privacy & Security: What Data Leaves Your Machine?

  • All task data transits Zapier — their SOC 2 and GDPR posture is mature, review the DPA
  • Task history retains payload data — set retention windows for sensitive flows
  • No-training stance on customer data; confirm current policy
  • Enterprise adds SSO, restricted access and audit logging

FAQ

Is Zapier free to use?

Yes. The free plan includes 100 tasks per month with 2-step zaps — enough to test basic automations. Paid plans from $19.99/month unlock multi-step zaps and higher task volumes.

Is Zapier better than Make?

Zapier is easier to start with and has the largest app ecosystem, plus the AI Agents feature. Make offers a visual flow builder with more complex logic and better volume pricing. Choose Zapier for simplicity and integrations; choose Make for intricate automations at scale.

What can I automate with Zapier?

Anything involving two or more apps: routing leads from forms to CRMs, posting content to social channels, syncing data between tools, sending notifications, and AI-assisted workflows like summarizing new support tickets. With 8,000+ integrations, most business processes can be automated.

When does Zapier become too expensive?

Roughly past 10-20k tasks/month — at that volume, Make or self-hosted n8n deliver the same workflows for a fraction of the cost. Under that, Zapier’s ease is worth it.

What are Zapier AI actions?

AI-powered steps inside zaps: text classification, data extraction, drafting and formatting using LLMs — plus ChatGPT/Claude integrations as standard steps.
