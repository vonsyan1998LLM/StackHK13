What is Synthesia?

Synthesia is the most established AI avatar video platform, used by 60,000+ companies including half the Fortune 100. It turns a script into a presenter-led video using one of 230+ AI avatars in 140+ languages — no cameras, no studios, no actors.

What separates Synthesia from the pack in 2026 is its enterprise focus: compliance certifications, SSO, bulk API video generation, and an editing experience built for corporate learning teams, not just marketers.

Pricing

  • Starter ($29/month): 1 editor, 10 min of video, watermark-free, 1 avatar
  • Creator ($89/month): 3 editors, 30 min/month, unlimited avatars, more languages
  • Enterprise (custom): API, SSO, custom avatars, unlimited usage

Synthesia is priced as a professional tool. The Creator tier at $89/month is where it gets serious, but for a single marketing video or a course, Starter is a viable entry — unlike HeyGen's more forgiving free tier, you'll be paying to really evaluate it.

Who Should Use It

Perfect for: Enterprises producing training and onboarding videos at scale, teams that need multi-language localization, and anyone who needs compliance-friendly AI video.

Not ideal for: Budget-conscious solo creators — HeyGen or Descript offer better value — or teams needing highly stylized, cinematic output (see Runway).

FAQ

Is Synthesia better than HeyGen?

For enterprise use, yes — Synthesia's avatar realism, compliance and API are more mature. HeyGen wins on price and accessibility for individuals. Choose Synthesia for scale and compliance, HeyGen for quick, affordable videos.

Does Synthesia have a free trial?

Synthesia offers a limited free video preview and a demo, but full production requires a paid plan starting at $29/month. It's a paid platform from day one — unlike HeyGen's more generous trial.

Can Synthesia make videos in other languages?

Yes — one of its biggest strengths. It supports 140+ languages with automatic lip-sync matching, making it the best tool we've tested for translating a single video into many localized versions.

Synthesia vs HeyGen?

Synthesia for enterprise training/localization at scale (governance, SOC2, LMS export); HeyGen for marketing/social avatar video with faster creative iteration. Both are credible — audience decides.

Can I create a custom avatar of myself?

Yes — a short studio-style recording trains a personal avatar with your voice in 40+ languages. The process takes a recording session and a review cycle.
