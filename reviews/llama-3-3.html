Risks We Found During Testing

Beyond the standard "AI hallucinates" caveat, here are the specific risks we encountered:

  • Self-hosting requires significant compute for large variants
  • Quality trails frontier closed models on complex reasoning
  • Open weights can be fine-tuned for misuse
  • Safety guardrails depend on implementation, not built-in

Privacy & Security: What Data Leaves Your Machine?

  • Self-hosted = full data control
  • Open weights auditable for bias and safety
  • API providers (Groq, Together) have separate privacy policies
  • No telemetry in self-hosted mode
