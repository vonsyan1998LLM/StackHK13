What is Gamma?

Gamma is an AI-powered presentation tool that generates complete, polished decks — slides, layouts, charts, and visuals — from a text prompt or outline. It's designed for people who need professional-looking presentations fast, without wrestling with PowerPoint or designing from scratch.

Unlike traditional slide tools, Gamma handles the entire design process. You provide the content or idea, and it produces a cohesive, modern deck you can refine and present immediately.

Risks We Found During Testing

Beyond the standard "AI hallucinates" caveat, here are the specific risks we encountered:

  • PPT/PDF export fidelity limited for complex layouts
  • Data charts from prompts require manual verification
  • Card-based editing model may not suit traditional slide workflows
  • Web-first format means print layouts need adjustment

Privacy & Security: What Data Leaves Your Machine?

  • Decks are stored on Gamma servers and shareable via link by default
  • Generated content is not used for model training (per privacy policy)
  • Export to PPT/PDF processes data through Gamma servers
  • Team workspaces available for collaborative editing

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Gamma free to use?

Gamma has a free tier with limited AI credits and a set number of presentations, plus paid plans for more generations and premium features.

Can Gamma replace PowerPoint?

For most use cases, yes. Gamma generates polished, modern decks much faster than PowerPoint, though PowerPoint still offers more granular manual control.

Is Gamma good for non-designers?

Absolutely — Gamma's main appeal is that anyone can create professional-looking presentations without design skills. You describe the content and it handles the layout.

How does the scoring work?

Our scoring is based on: Features (30%), Ease of Use (20%), Performance (25%), Value (15%), and Support (10%). Each tool is tested for 2-4 weeks of real-world use before scoring.

Can Gamma export to PowerPoint?

Yes — PPT and PDF export are available, but complex layouts may not translate perfectly. Web export is the native format and preserves all interactive elements.

Is Gamma free to use?

Gamma has a generous free tier (400 credits to start, unlimited basic decks). Paid plans unlock custom branding, unlimited AI generation and advanced export options.
