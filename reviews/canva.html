What is Canva AI?

Canva is the world's most popular design platform, and its Magic Studio layer adds generative AI across the entire product. From a blank page, you can now describe an idea and get a finished social post, presentation or thumbnail — complete with AI-generated imagery, copy and layout.

The key tools: Magic Design (prompt → full design), Magic Write (AI copy for your design), Magic Media (text-to-image and video), Magic Eraser (object removal), and one-click background removal. It's design-for-everyone, supercharged.

FAQ

Is Canva AI free?

Basic Magic tools are available free with limited credits. The full Magic Studio — including Magic Design, Magic Write and background removal — requires Canva Pro at $12.99/month.

Is Canva AI good for professional design?

For marketing and content design, absolutely. For precise, high-end creative work, dedicated tools like Leonardo AI or Adobe Firefly offer deeper control. Canva excels at speed and accessibility.

Does Canva AI own the images it creates?

Yes — content created with Magic Studio is owned by you and covered by Canva's content license terms, including commercial use for paying customers. This makes it safer than many public generators for business content.

Is Canva Pro worth it for a small business?

If anyone on the team designs weekly (social, decks, print), yes — Brand Kits, Magic Studio and the template library typically replace ad-hoc design spend quickly.

Canva vs Figma?

Different jobs: Canva for fast marketing collateral by non-designers; Figma for interface/product design by designers. Many teams run both.
