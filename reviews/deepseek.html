DeepSeek Review

The Open-Source AI That Changed Pricing

Quick Verdict

DeepSeek is the most disruptive AI release in years. This open-source model family delivers ChatGPT-level reasoning at a fraction of the price — often 90% cheaper via API. If you care about cost, control, or self-hosting, DeepSeek is a must-try. The chat app is free, the quality is genuinely impressive, and the open weights mean you're never locked in.

What is DeepSeek?

DeepSeek is an open-source AI assistant and model family developed by DeepSeek (a Chinese AI lab). Unlike most commercial chatbots, DeepSeek publishes its model weights openly, allowing anyone to use, self-host, or fine-tune them. The flagship chat app is free, and the API is priced dramatically below the market.

What made DeepSeek famous was the "DeepSeek moment" — a breakthrough showing that open models could match closed frontier models at a tiny fraction of the cost. It's become the default choice for developers, researchers, and cost-conscious teams worldwide.

Reasoning & Intelligence

DeepSeek's reasoning capabilities genuinely surprised us. On math, logic, and coding benchmarks it consistently rivals (and sometimes beats) models priced 10x higher. The "think" mode walks through problems step-by-step, producing transparent reasoning chains that are easy to verify.

"We switched our internal research pipeline from a $20/month commercial API to DeepSeek and cut API costs by 92%. Output quality for our use case — structured data extraction and summarization — was indistinguishable."

Long-context handling is another strength. DeepSeek processes large documents comfortably, making it strong for codebase analysis and research synthesis.

Key Features

Open Weights

The biggest differentiator. Models are released openly, so you can self-host, fine-tune, or audit them. No vendor lock-in, full data control, and the entire developer community can build on top of them.

DeepThink / Reasoning Mode

A chain-of-thought mode that breaks complex problems into verifiable steps. It's slower than normal mode but dramatically more accurate on math, logic, and planning tasks.

File & Document Upload

Upload PDFs, images, and documents for analysis. DeepSeek reads them and answers with source awareness — handy for research and document processing.

API & Self-Hosting

A dirt-cheap OpenAI-compatible API plus official self-hosting options. Developers can drop it in with minimal code changes while controlling their own infrastructure.

Pricing

This is where DeepSeek wins the market. The chat app is completely free. The API is priced at a fraction of OpenAI and Anthropic — often $0.50–$2 per million tokens depending on the model, versus $15–$60 for comparable frontier models.

Risks We Found During Testing

Beyond the standard "AI hallucinates" caveat, here are the specific risks we encountered:

  • Open-weight model means self-hosting requires technical expertise
  • China-based company — data residency concerns for regulated industries
  • Newer model versions may introduce regressions not caught in testing
  • English output quality occasionally trails Chinese output quality

Privacy & Security: What Data Leaves Your Machine?

  • API data handling policies differ from self-hosted deployments
  • Self-hosted deployments give full data control — no data leaves your infrastructure
  • Cloud API data may be processed in China depending on endpoint
  • Open-weight model can be audited (unlike closed-source rivals)

Frequently Asked Questions

Is DeepSeek free to use?

Yes, the DeepSeek chat app is free to use, and the API is priced dramatically below OpenAI's — often 90-95% cheaper for comparable output quality.

Is DeepSeek better than ChatGPT?

For reasoning and math tasks, DeepSeek matches or exceeds ChatGPT in several benchmarks at a fraction of the cost. For creative writing and image generation, ChatGPT remains stronger. It depends on your use case.

Is DeepSeek really open-source?

Yes. DeepSeek releases its model weights openly under an MIT-compatible license, allowing anyone to self-host or fine-tune the models.

How does the scoring work?

Is DeepSeek V4 as good as GPT-5.6?

For coding and reasoning benchmarks, DeepSeek V4-Pro performs competitively with GPT-5.6 at a fraction of the cost. For creative writing and multimodal tasks, GPT-5.6 still leads. The self-hostable open-weight version is unique.

Can I self-host DeepSeek?

Yes — the open-weight model can be self-hosted via the V4-Pro weights. Requires significant GPU infrastructure for the full model; distilled versions run on smaller hardware.
