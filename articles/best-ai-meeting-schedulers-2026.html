FAQ

Which AI meeting scheduler is best in 2026?

Motion is the best overall for automated scheduling and task re-planning. Reclaim is the best for protecting deep-work time, Clockwise the best for whole-team optimization, and Calendly remains the dependable choice for simple booking links.

Do AI schedulers really save time?

In our four-week test, Motion and Reclaim each saved roughly 3-4 hours of calendar management per week per user, mostly by eliminating manual rescheduling and defending focus blocks. The saving scales with how meeting-heavy your week is.

How much do AI meeting schedulers cost?

Calendly and Clockwise offer useful free tiers, with paid plans from about $10-12 per user per month. Reclaim starts around $12, and Motion's full automation runs about $34 per user per month. Team adoption matters more than price.

Do AI schedulers work with both Google and Microsoft calendars?

Reclaim, Motion and Clockwise are strongest in the Google Calendar ecosystem, while Calendly handles both gracefully and Hootsuite-adjacent tools skew Microsoft. Check your primary calendar before committing — the tools that feel magical in one ecosystem can be frustrating in the other.

Will an AI scheduler book over my existing commitments?

The good ones protect existing commitments by default. Motion and Reclaim both kept our fixed meetings intact and scheduled around them, and both default to defending focus blocks. The key is spending the 10-minute setup defining what must never move.
