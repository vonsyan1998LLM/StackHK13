1. Deel — Best Global Coverage

1

Deel — Best Global Coverage

EOR and payroll in 150+ countries with real compliance depth
EOR · Global payroll · Immigration · Contractor management · ~$549/EOR/mo
Deel remains the deepest global employment platform: contracts, local compliance and payroll in 150+ countries, with 24/7 support staffed by specialists. Onboarding a Philippines EOR employee took under 10 minutes in our test.

2. Rippling — Best Unified Platform

2

Rippling — Best Unified Platform

HR, payroll, IT and spend on one employee graph
HR + IT unified · Workflow automation · Global EOR · from ~$8/user/mo
Rippling’s employee graph is the operational advantage: change a role and payroll, apps, devices and benefits update together. US-payroll is its deep layer; global coverage runs through EOR. For companies that also want IT management, it’s unmatched.

3. BambooHR — Best Simple HR Core

3

BambooHR — Best Simple HR Core

The easiest HRIS for small-to-mid companies
HRIS · Time off · Reporting · ~$10/user/mo est.
BambooHR does the HR fundamentals — records, time off, onboarding checklists, reporting — without overwhelming small teams. AI features are lighter (helpdesk bot, anomaly flags); global payroll requires partners.

4. Gusto — Best US Small-Business Payroll

4

Gusto — Best US Small-Business Payroll

Full-service US payroll from ~$40/mo base
US payroll · Benefits · HR lite · ~$40/mo + $6/user
Gusto is the friendliest full-service US payroll: taxes filed automatically, benefits administration included, and a flat price structure tiny teams can predict. International coverage is minimal — US-only payroll with contractor payments abroad.

FAQ

Deel vs Rippling for international teams?

Deel for EOR depth across 150+ countries and contractor-first workflows; Rippling when you also want IT/app/device management unified with HR. Scale-ups often run both.

Which HR platform is best under 10 employees?

Gusto — flat pricing (~$40/mo base), excellent US payroll and benefits, with the least administrative overhead for very small teams.

Do AI HR features actually help?

The useful ones are practical: policy Q&A bots, onboarding copilots and anomaly flags on payroll. Treat anything labeled “AI-powered” that just summarizes text as marketing.
