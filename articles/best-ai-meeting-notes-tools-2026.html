FAQ

Are AI meeting notes accurate?

On clear audio, 90-95% transcription accuracy is standard across the top tools. Heavy accents, crosstalk and bad microphones are the main accuracy killers — the speaker labeling held up better than raw word accuracy in all four tools we tested.

Is it legal to record meetings with AI notetakers?

With participant consent, yes in most jurisdictions — but consent rules vary (two-party consent states/countries require everyone to agree). Announce the bot, and check your region’s rules before rolling out team-wide.

Which AI meeting tool has the best free plan?

Fathom is the standout free option (unlimited recordings with core features), with Fireflies close behind. Fathom’s free tier is why it keeps winning Reddit recommendations.
