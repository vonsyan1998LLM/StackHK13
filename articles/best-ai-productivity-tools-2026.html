FAQ

Which single AI tool gives the most productivity boost?

ChatGPT-4o offers the broadest utility — writing, research, data analysis, coding help, and brainstorming in one tool. For teams already in Notion, the Notion AI add-on at $10/month is the highest-ROI option since it integrates where you already work.

Are AI productivity tools worth the subscription cost?

For knowledge workers billing $50-150/hour, saving just 15 minutes a day justifies a $20/month subscription. Our testing found average time savings of 3-7 hours per week across the tools on this list when integrated into daily workflows.

Can I use free versions instead of paid AI tools?

Free tiers (ChatGPT free, Claude free, Perplexity free) are surprisingly capable for casual use. The jump to paid ($20/month) primarily gives you higher usage limits, faster responses, and access to the most capable models. Start free, upgrade when you hit limits.
