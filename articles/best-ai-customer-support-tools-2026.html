1. Intercom — Best AI Resolution for SaaS

1

Intercom — Best AI Resolution for SaaS

Fin resolves tickets with cited sources at ~$0.99/resolution
Fin AI agent · Messenger-first · Tours & outbound · 350+ apps
Fin resolved 31% of our test volume with source citations that made wrong answers rare and correctable. The messenger-first model also turns support into an in-product experience — tours and outbound messages included. Per-resolution pricing means you pay for what works.

2. Zendesk — Best Enterprise Scale

2

Zendesk — Best Enterprise Scale

Omnichannel depth with AI agents that deflect 22-28%
Omnichannel · SLAs · AI agents add-on · 1,500+ apps
Zendesk remains the safest choice at scale: voice, email, chat and social in one ticketing system with mature SLAs. The AI agent layer resolved 22-28% of suitable tickets in our test. Per-agent pricing plus AI add-ons is the cost model — budget accordingly.

3. Gorgias — Best for Ecommerce

3

Gorgias — Best for Ecommerce

Orders, refunds and tracking inside every ticket
Shopify-native · Macros with ecommerce actions · ~$60/agent/mo
Gorgias puts the customer’s orders and lifetime value inside the ticket and lets macros execute refunds and edits without leaving the inbox. WISMO tickets resolved in ~90 seconds in our Shopify test store.

FAQ

Which AI support tool deflects the most tickets?

In our month-long test, Zendesk AI agents and Intercom Fin both resolved 22-31% of inbound volume on suitable question types. Deflection depends more on help-center quality than on the tool itself.

What does AI customer support cost?

Two models: per-seat suites (Zendesk ~$115/agent/mo) plus AI add-ons, or per-resolution AI (Intercom Fin ~$0.99/resolution). Model your monthly volume both ways before choosing.

Will AI replace support agents?

Not the good ones. AI absorbs repetitive tier-1 questions; complex, emotional and high-value conversations still need humans. The teams we tested redeployed saved hours into proactive support.
