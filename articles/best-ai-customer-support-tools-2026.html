FAQ

Which AI support tool deflects the most tickets?

In our month-long test, Zendesk AI agents and Intercom Fin both resolved 22-31% of inbound volume on suitable question types. Deflection depends more on help-center quality than on the tool itself.

What does AI customer support cost?

Two models: per-seat suites (Zendesk ~$115/agent/mo) plus AI add-ons, or per-resolution AI (Intercom Fin ~$0.99/resolution). Model your monthly volume both ways before choosing.

Will AI replace support agents?

Not the good ones. AI absorbs repetitive tier-1 questions; complex, emotional and high-value conversations still need humans. The teams we tested redeployed saved hours into proactive support.

How to Choose: A Decision Framework

Choosing among these Ai Customer Support Tools options is easier when you sequence the decision. First, name the single workflow that justifies the purchase — not the wish list, the one job that hurts today. Second, check that the pick labeled for that job fits your stack: integrations are where enthusiasm meets reality. Third, price your realistic usage on the vendor's own calculator, including the usage-based features you will actually trigger. Finally, run the free tier or trial against real work for a week before any annual commitment. Teams that follow this sequence rarely regret their choice; teams that skip straight to feature comparisons churn tools quarterly.

Common Mistakes When Choosing

The mistakes we see most often, in order of cost: buying for features nobody uses, which is pure waste; skipping the trial because the free tier "seems fine", which hides the real workflow until after the invoice; over-buying seats before measuring usage; and ignoring exit paths, which turns a modest subscription into a lock-in problem. A fifth, quieter mistake: choosing the tool your competitor uses. Their workflow is not your workflow, and their purchase was probably a mistake too.
