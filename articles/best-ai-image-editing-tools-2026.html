FAQ

Which AI image editor is best for beginners?

Canva — its Magic Studio tools (background remover, Magic Edit, Magic Eraser) work in one click and Brand Kits keep everything on-brand automatically.

Leonardo AI vs Adobe Firefly?

Leonardo for creative control, style models and generous free daily tokens; Firefly for commercial safety (Adobe’s licensing indemnification) and Creative Cloud integration.

Do AI edits hold up in print?

At 2x resolution with proper export settings, yes for marketing collateral. For large-format print, generate at maximum resolution and upscale with a dedicated tool.
