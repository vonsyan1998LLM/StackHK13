How We Tested

Each platform reviewed the same 12 contracts — an MSA, an SPA, three NDAs, two employment agreements, two SaaS terms, a lease and two vendor contracts — plus 8 research briefs. We graded clause accuracy, hallucination rate, citation verifiability, speed per document and cost per matter over four weeks on paid accounts.

1. Harvey — Best Overall

1

Harvey — Best Overall

Matter-depth reasoning with verifiable citations
Contract analysis · Research briefs · Citation trail · Matter memory
Harvey's edge is depth: it treats a whole matter as context, so redlines stay consistent with prior positions and every answer carries a citation you can check. It is the closest thing to a junior associate we tested. On the research side it also assembled a jurisdiction memo with authority-flagged citations in under four minutes, a task our mid-level associate budgeted at two hours. The depth comes at a price, but for the firms that can afford it, the leverage is real.

2. Luminance — Best for High-Volume Review

2

Luminance — Best for High-Volume Review

Pattern engine for contract portfolios
Portfolio scanning · Deviation clustering · Data rooms
Luminance won the volume test outright: 200 contracts in, it clustered deviations we flagged as real risks faster than any human team. For deal work it is the strongest tool in the category. Its deviation reports group contracts by risk pattern rather than listing every change, which is exactly how a reviewer wants to triage a data room. It also handled mixed-format archives — PDFs, scans, legacy exports — without a cleanup pass, which saved us a full day of pre-processing.

3. Spellbook — Best Value

3

Spellbook — Best Value

Word-native drafting for everyday practice
Inline Word drafting · Redlines · Clause library
Spellbook meets lawyers where they work. The drafting is strong, the price is right, and the onboarding is measured in minutes rather than weeks — the practical pick for most small firms. The clause library also brought consistency to repeat drafting: once we saved our standard positions, new matters inherited them automatically. That compounding effect is why adoption inside a firm accelerates after the first month.

4. Lexis+ AI — Best Research

Lexis+ AI — Best Research

Authority-flagged legal research
Case & statute search · Citation flags · Jurisdiction answers
Lexis+ AI is a research specialist. It does not draft like Harvey, but its citation discipline is unmatched — every proposition traces to authority, which is what wins motions. The citation discipline extended to cross-jurisdiction queries, where it flagged authority that applied in one state and not another — exactly the kind of trap a general web search misses. It is less a drafting tool and more a research safety net.

Which Should You Pick?

<strong>Firms with deal work</strong> should start with Luminance for volume and add Harvey for the substantive matters. <strong>Solos and small firms</strong> get the best return from Spellbook. <strong>Litigators</strong> should lean on Lexis+ AI for research and pair it with a drafting tool.

Our pick: Harvey for depth, Luminance for volume, Spellbook for value — most practices should buy one drafting tool and one research tool rather than a single do-everything platform. 9.1/10 for the top pick.

FAQ

Which AI legal tool is best in 2026?

Harvey is the best overall for substantive contract and research work, with verifiable citations and matter-level context. Luminance is the volume pick, Spellbook the value pick, and Lexis+ AI the research specialist.

Is AI legal research reliable enough for client work?

In our testing, Harvey and Lexis+ AI produced verifiable citations on the vast majority of queries, but every platform still needs a human check — treat AI output as a first draft that a lawyer must verify before filing or advising.

How much do AI legal tools cost?

Spellbook starts around $35 per user per month, Lexis+ AI is an add-on to a Lexis subscription, and Luminance and Harvey are enterprise-priced with custom quotes usually above $50-100 per user per month.
