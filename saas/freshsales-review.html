What is Freshsales?

Freshsales is part of the Freshworks suite (alongside Freshdesk and Freshservice) — a CRM with built-in phone, email and chat, an AI assistant named Freddy for deal insights, and autoscoring that ranks leads by engagement.

Its pitch is unified comms without add-on pricing: where HubSpot gates calling and sequences behind tiers, Freshsales includes them from the free plan up.

Performance in Daily Use

The built-in dialer is the standout: click-to-call from any contact, automatic call logging with recordings attached to the timeline. Across 60+ calls we had zero dropped sessions. Freddy’s deal insights (“no activity in 14 days — risk”) were directionally right in most checks.

Email sequences work but lack the deliverability tooling of dedicated senders — high-volume outbound still wants Apollo or Instantly upstream.

Integrations & Ecosystem

The Freshworks marketplace covers the essentials (Zapier, Shopify, Slack, QuickBooks, Xero) but is noticeably smaller than HubSpot’s. Native pairing with Freshdesk is the strategic play — support and sales share context cleanly.

Support & Documentation

Chat support answered within minutes in our tests, even on the free plan (rare). Documentation is organized but shallower — advanced configuration questions often end at “contact support.”

Pricing

  • Free: Core CRM for small teams — leads, contacts, deals and basic Freddy features
  • Growth (~$9/user/mo): Auto profile enrichment, sequences, 2-way phone
  • Pro (~$39/user/mo): Freddy AI insights, advanced permissions
  • Enterprise (~$59/user/mo): Audit logs, IP ranges, dedicated manager

Freshsales frequently bundles promotions (free Growth for 3-6 months). Confirm current tiers — Freshworks revises packaging more often than competitors. Prices as of August 2026.

Who Should Use It

Perfect for: small businesses that want calling, email and AI insights in one affordable CRM — especially teams already using or considering Freshdesk.

Not ideal for: scaling sales orgs that need deep reporting, large marketplaces and revenue-forecasting depth (HubSpot, Salesforce or Pipedrive territory).

Final Verdict: Freshsales is the best value CRM in its price band: built-in comms and included AI make the free and Growth tiers feel above their price. Depth is where it trades — reporting and ecosystem trail HubSpot. For SMB pipelines, an easy recommendation. 8.1/10.

FAQ

Is Freshsales really free?

There is a permanent free plan with core CRM, contact lifecycle stages and basic Freddy features for small teams. Phone minutes and advanced automation kick in on Growth.

Freshsales vs HubSpot for a small team?

Freshsales wins on built-in phone/email cost and simplicity; HubSpot wins on ecosystem, reporting and content marketing tools. At 1-5 users the free plans of both are worth trialing side by side.

How good is the Freddy AI assistant?

Useful, not magical: deal-insight flags (stalled deals, engagement drops) and lead scoring are directionally accurate after tuning. Forecasting is lighter than dedicated revenue-intelligence tools.

Does the built-in phone work internationally?

Calling covers most major markets with per-minute or bundle pricing; check country rates for your call destinations. Recording and transcription are included on paid tiers.
