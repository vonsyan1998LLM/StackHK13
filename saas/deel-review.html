What is Deel?

Deel is a global payroll and HR platform that helps companies hire, pay, and manage international talent. It offers contractor payments (pay freelancers in 150+ countries), global payroll (run compliant payroll in 100+ countries), and EOR services (employ workers anywhere without setting up a local entity).

Its value is removing the compliance headache of international hiring. Deel handles contracts, taxes, and local labor laws, letting you focus on work rather than paperwork.

FAQ

How much does Deel cost?

Contractor payments start at $49/month per contractor. Global payroll starts at $29/month plus per-employee fees. EOR services start at around $599/month per employee. Costs scale with team size.

What is EOR (Employer of Record)?

EOR lets you employ workers in countries where you have no legal entity. Deel becomes the legal employer on paper, handling payroll, taxes, and compliance, while you manage the day-to-day work. It's the fastest way to hire full-time globally.

Is Deel safe and reliable for payments?

Yes. Deel is one of the most widely used global payroll platforms, trusted by thousands of companies. It offers multiple payment methods, compliance guarantees, and strong security. Contractor payments are fast and reliable across 150+ countries.

How much does Deel cost per contractor or employee?

Contractor plans start at ~$49/contractor/month; EOR (full employment) runs ~$549/employee/month plus local fees. Mass contractor plans and annual pricing reduce per-head costs.

Deel vs Rippling for global teams?

Deel for pure global EOR/contractor coverage (more countries, deeper compliance); Rippling when you also want US payroll, devices and internal IT in one platform. Both are excellent — scope decides.
