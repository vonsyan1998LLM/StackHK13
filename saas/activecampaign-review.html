What is ActiveCampaign?

ActiveCampaign is an email marketing and automation platform known for its powerful visual automation builder. You can create complex customer journeys with triggers, conditions, and actions — without coding. It also includes a CRM, landing pages, and SMS marketing.

Its sweet spot is depth at a reasonable price: you get HubSpot-level automation capability without the enterprise price tag, making it a favorite of growing businesses.

How We Tested

  • Built 5 automation workflows — welcome, win-back, and behavioral triggers
  • Ran email campaigns and split tests for a month
  • Compared directly against HubSpot and Mailchimp

Cons

Pricing

  • Starter ($15/month): Email marketing, basic automation
  • Plus ($49/month): Full automation, CRM, landing pages
  • Pro ($149/month): Predictive sending, advanced reporting

ActiveCampaign pricing scales by contact count. The Plus tier is where it becomes a true marketing automation platform. For serious automation needs, it's typically cheaper than HubSpot.

Who Should Use It

Perfect for: e-commerce and B2B marketers who need sophisticated automation without enterprise budgets, and teams that outgrew Mailchimp's simplicity.

Not ideal for: total beginners (Mailchimp is easier), or large enterprises needing HubSpot's full platform and ecosystem breadth.

Final Verdict: ActiveCampaign remains the best value in marketing automation. Its automation builder is genuinely powerful, and it delivers HubSpot-level capability at a fraction of the price. The interface isn't the prettiest, but for results-driven marketers, it's a top pick. 8.7/10.

FAQ

Is ActiveCampaign good for beginners?

It has a learning curve. Total beginners may find Mailchimp simpler. But if you're willing to learn, ActiveCampaign's automation power pays off quickly. The 14-day trial is a good way to evaluate.

ActiveCampaign vs HubSpot — which is better?

HubSpot is better if you need the full CRM + marketing + sales platform and a free CRM. ActiveCampaign is better value if your focus is email automation and you don't need HubSpot's breadth. Compare based on your stack.

Is ActiveCampaign worth the price?

For marketers using real automation (not just broadcast email), yes. The ability to build behavioral journeys that Mailchimp can't easily replicate makes it worth the cost for most growing businesses.
