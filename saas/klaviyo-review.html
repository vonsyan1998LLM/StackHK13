What is Klaviyo?

Klaviyo is an ecommerce-native marketing platform: every profile carries full order history, browsing behavior and predicted metrics (CLV, churn risk, next order date) out of the box. Email and SMS flows build directly on Shopify events — no CSV wrangling, no sync lag.

Its advantage is data depth turned segmentation: "high CLV customers who haven’t ordered in 60 days" is a two-click segment, and the flows that target it are revenue, not vanity.

Performance in Daily Use

The headline number from our 60-day test: attributed revenue of ~$11,400 from email and SMS flows alone (18% of store revenue), with abandoned-checkout and win-back flows doing the heavy lifting. Predictive analytics performed: churn-risk targeting recovered customers at 2.4× the rate of blanket discounts.

The flow builder’s conditional splits (value of last order, predicted gender, coupon status) are what Mailchimp simply cannot express.

Integrations & Ecosystem

Shopify integration is the deepest in the industry (Klaviyo was built for it), with near-real-time event sync. BigCommerce, WooCommerce and Adobe Commerce are supported; 350+ apps including Recharge, Gorgias and Justuno.

The review/survey products (Klaviyo Reviews) are newer but competent — one less subscription for DTC brands.

Support & Documentation

Chat support responds in minutes on paid tiers and is genuinely knowledgeable about ecommerce. Klaviyo Academy and the template library short-circuit the learning curve; the community (Facebook groups, agency playbooks) is the largest in ecommerce email.

Pricing

  • Email: Usage-based — ~$45/mo at 1,500 profiles
  • Email + SMS: Usage-based — combined profiles, shared billing

The cost curve is the main objection: at 25,000+ mostly-inactive profiles, cleaning lists (or moving dormant ones out) is part of operating Klaviyo well. Prices as of August 2026.

Who Should Use It

Perfect for: DTC ecommerce brands on Shopify (or BigCommerce/WooCommerce) doing meaningful revenue, who want segmentation and flows that generate measurable revenue.

Not ideal for: B2B, SaaS or content businesses — Klaviyo’s data model assumes orders and carts. General newsletters belong in Mailchimp or ConvertKit.

Final Verdict: Klaviyo is the best ecommerce email and SMS platform in 2026, full stop. The Shopify-native data model and predictive segmentation turn email from a newsletter into a revenue channel. The pricing curve is real — and still worth it. 8.8/10.

Reliability Notes From Long-Term Testing

Deliverability was the metric that mattered across our test month, and Klaviyo held up: campaigns landed consistently, engagement tracking was accurate against our control sends, and automation journeys triggered on time. The learning curve is real but front-loaded — the first campaign setup takes an afternoon, the twentieth takes minutes.

Who Should Skip It

Skip Klaviyo if you send only occasional newsletters — a lightweight free tool covers that job, and you'd be paying for automation machinery you never start. E-commerce teams with tiny lists should also do the math on credits versus revenue; the platform earns its keep at meaningful send volume.

FAQ

Is Klaviyo worth it for a small store?

Below ~$5k/month revenue, start with the free tier and 2-3 core flows. The paid jump pays off when email/SMS drives $2k+/month — the attribution dashboard makes this visible.

Klaviyo vs Mailchimp for Shopify?

Klaviyo, clearly, for any serious Shopify store: native data, better flows, predictive segments and ecommerce-specific benchmarks. Mailchimp’s Shopify integration is maintained but shallower.

Why does Klaviyo get expensive?

Billing is by active profiles (anyone you can email/SMS). Large lists of one-time buyers inflate cost — solve it with list hygiene and flow-based suppression, which Klaviyo tools support natively.

Does Klaviyo support SMS outside the US?

SMS sending covers US, CA, UK, AU and major EU markets; other regions are email-first. Check current country coverage and local compliance before planning an SMS-heavy strategy.

Will Klaviyo improve my deliverability?

The platform provides the infrastructure (dedicated IP options, authentication, reputation monitoring), but deliverability is earned through sending practice: list hygiene, engaged audiences and consistent volume. Tools help; content and list quality decide.

List pricing is the beginning, not the total.
