What is GetResponse?

GetResponse is a full marketing platform that bundles email marketing, marketing automation, landing pages, forms, and webinars. Where many tools focus on one channel, GetResponse aims to cover a marketer's entire funnel — from list building to automated nurture to live webinars.

Its defining strength is automation depth at a budget price. A visual automation builder, combined with landing pages and webinars, means you can run serious campaigns without buying three separate tools.

GetResponse's pricing is contact-based and aggressively competitive. For the feature set — automation, landing pages, webinars — the value per dollar is among the best in email marketing.

Who Should Use It

Perfect for: solo marketers, coaches, and growing teams that want email, automation, and landing pages in one budget-friendly subscription.

Not ideal for: teams that prefer the polish and ecosystem of ActiveCampaign or Mailchimp, or stores needing deep ecommerce-specific automation like Omnisend provides.

FAQ

Is GetResponse worth it in 2026?

Yes, especially for budget-conscious marketers. You get email, automation, landing pages, and webinars in one subscription starting at $19/month — a combination few competitors match at the price. Just be ready for an interface that lags behind the newest tools.

Is GetResponse better than Mailchimp?

For serious marketing automation, yes — GetResponse's visual automation builder is far more capable than Mailchimp's, and it bundles landing pages and webinars. Mailchimp is friendlier for absolute beginners and has a larger template ecosystem.

Does GetResponse include landing pages?

Yes. GetResponse includes a landing page builder with responsive templates in most plans, plus forms and popups to drive signups. Higher tiers unlock more pages and the ability to connect custom domains.

GetResponse vs Mailchimp in 2026?

GetResponse for webinars, automation depth and 24/7 chat support at lower cost. Mailchimp for brand trust, nicer templates and landing pages — if you accept the contact-based pricing model.

Is the free plan real?

The free plan covers 500 contacts with core email + a website builder — enough to evaluate properly. Webinars require a paid tier (plus starts around $19/mo at small list sizes).
