What is Rippling?

Rippling is an employee management platform built on a single data graph: HR, payroll, benefits, spend and device management share one source of truth. Hire someone and their payroll, laptop, app accounts and benefits provision automatically — the “workforce OS” pitch is real.

Its differentiator vs Deel is breadth beyond HR: Rippling also replaces the IT stack (device management, SSO, app provisioning), which Deel leaves to other tools.

FAQ

Rippling vs Deel — which for global hiring?

Deel for EOR depth in more countries and contractor-first workflows; Rippling when you also want IT/app/device management and US payroll in the same platform. Many scale-ups run Deel for EOR + Rippling for internal HR/IT.

How does Rippling pricing work?

A base platform fee per employee, plus per-module fees (payroll, benefits, IT, spend). Total cost depends on enabled modules — request an itemized quote and compare against your current tool stack.

Can Rippling manage laptops and app access?

Yes — device management (macOS/Windows/Linux), SSO and automated app provisioning are core modules: hire someone and accounts/devices set up automatically; offboard and access revokes in one action.

Does Rippling work outside the US?

Global payroll runs through Rippling’s EOR and contractor products (150+ countries); its own payroll engine is deepest in the US. Non-US entities should compare EOR terms with Deel/Remote.
