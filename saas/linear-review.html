What is Linear?

Linear is an opinionated issue tracker and project planner for product teams: sub-50ms interactions, keyboard-driven workflows, cycles for sprints, roadmaps for leadership, and tight GitHub/GitLab/Figma integrations. 2026 added AI triage that drafts issue descriptions and routes duplicates.

Its philosophy is constraining on purpose — fewer settings, strong defaults — which is exactly why teams migrate from Jira and never look back.

FAQ

Is Linear free for small teams?

Yes — the free plan includes 250 issues, two teams and core features with no time limit. Most hobby and early-stage projects never pay.

Can Linear replace Jira?

For product teams up to ~100 engineers, yes — migration tools handle issues, comments and attachments. Enterprises needing advanced Jira governance should trial both with two real sprints.

What does Linear’s AI do?

It drafts well-structured issue descriptions from rough notes, detects duplicates, suggests labels and teams for triage, and summarizes cycle activity. It works quietly rather than as a chatbot.
