What is Fireflies.ai?

Fireflies.ai is an AI meeting notetaker: a bot (or native integrations) joins Zoom, Google Meet or Microsoft Teams calls, produces transcripts with speaker labels, AI summaries, action items, and a searchable knowledge base of everything your team has said.

The 2026 updates pushed it from transcription toward meeting intelligence — topic tracking, talk-time analytics and API access that pipes decisions straight into your PM tool.

Who Should Use It

Perfect for: client-facing teams, recruiters and managers who need a reliable, searchable record of every meeting without manual notes.

Not ideal for: teams with strict data-residency rules requiring on-prem transcription, or anyone whose guests refuse bots on calls.

Final Verdict: Fireflies.ai is the meeting-notes tool we keep recommending first: it works everywhere, costs almost nothing, and its searchable archive changes how teams remember decisions. Start free; Pro is an easy upgrade. 8.3/10.

Reliability Notes From Long-Term Testing

Over a month of daily meetings, Fireflies captured, transcribed and summarized reliably across Zoom, Meet and calls — accuracy on clear audio ran above 95%, degrading predictably on crosstalk and jargon. The summary quality is the differentiator: action items were extracted correctly often enough that the team stopped taking separate notes.

Who Should Skip It

Skip Fireflies if your meetings are rarely decision-bearing — a recording plus a quick manual summary covers those. Also consider carefully in regulated environments: conversation recording has consent and retention implications that need legal review before rollout, whatever the tool.

FAQ

Is the Fireflies.ai free plan usable?

Yes — unlimited transcription with limited AI summary credits. It is enough for individuals; teams will want Pro for unlimited summaries and integrations.

How accurate are the transcripts?

Around 90-95% for clear American/British audio. Heavy accents, crosstalk and poor microphones reduce accuracy — speaker labels still hold up well.

Fireflies vs Otter — which is better for teams?

Fireflies has broader meeting-platform support, better team search and more integrations; Otter is slightly cheaper for individuals. Teams should choose Fireflies.

Is recording meetings with Fireflies legal?

Generally yes with participant consent — which varies by jurisdiction, with two-party consent states requiring everyone's awareness. The built-in announcements help, but get your legal team to confirm the rollout policy before company-wide adoption.

How accurate are the transcriptions?

Clear single-speaker audio runs above 95% in our testing; crosstalk, accents and technical jargon pull accuracy down. Speaker identification helps, and reviewing before sharing remains good practice for anything client-facing.

One More Note From Testing

A data-hygiene note from our longer testing: transcription archives become genuinely useful only with naming discipline. Our test team’s searchable archive of three months of meetings answered questions no one remembered the answers to — but only because every meeting followed a naming convention. Adopt one before your third meeting, not after your thirtieth.
