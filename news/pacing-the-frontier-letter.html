1,300+ employees ask Washington to help pace frontier AI

An open letter called "Pacing the Frontier" has drawn more than 1,300 signatures from employees of the four leading frontier AI labs — and, unusually, from their executives too.

The letter asks Washington for mechanisms to pace automated AI research and warns about systems that increasingly help conduct AI R&D themselves — the recursion scenario that has moved from papers to policy agendas. Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei is among the signatories, and OpenAI chief research officer Jakub Pachocki publicly cited his own signature in an August 19 statement.

Coverage has grown steadily since July, with signature counts rising from roughly 1,100 to beyond 1,300 as the letter circulated inside the labs. That governments, not companies, should hold the brakes on recursive self-improvement is a notable reversal of the usual industry posture — the people building frontier systems are asking to be slowed down.
