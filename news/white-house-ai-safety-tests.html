White House finalizes voluntary AI safety tests with the frontier labs

The US government finalized a set of voluntary AI safety tests in early August, with Meta, Anthropic, OpenAI and Google invited to meet White House officials on the framework’s rollout.

The arrangement stops short of regulation: labs submit frontier models for government-led evaluations on a voluntary basis, in exchange for a structured channel to regulators ahead of deployment. For Washington it is visibility into the frontier without legislation; for the labs it is predictability — and, critics would note, a framing that keeps mandatory rules off the table.

The meeting drew wide coverage precisely because all four frontier labs showed up. A shared testing channel with the US government lands differently in the same month that 1,300 of the labs’ own employees asked for stronger pacing mechanisms — pressure on the safety question is now arriving from regulators, staff and executives alike.
