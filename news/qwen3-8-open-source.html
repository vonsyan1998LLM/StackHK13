Alibaba open-sources Qwen3.8-2.4T — then puts a 27B on your laptop

Alibaba released Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B as open weights on August 13 — a 2.4-trillion-parameter flagship with 95B active parameters — and followed it on August 18 with Qwen3.8-27B, sized to run on consumer laptops.

The two releases bracket the open-weight market from both ends: a frontier-class MoE aimed at developers who need top-tier reasoning at API-beating economics, and a compact model that runs locally with a fraction of the memory. Together they make the Qwen line a full-stack answer to Meta’s open-weight franchise.

The cadence is the message. Five major Chinese open releases in eight weeks have turned model selection into a weekly re-evaluation for engineering teams — and per developer briefings, the download numbers suggest teams are actually re-evaluating rather than just bookmarking.
