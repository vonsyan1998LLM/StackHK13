OpenAI updates GPT-5.6 in ChatGPT — and Luna goes free

OpenAI rolled out an update to GPT-5.6 across ChatGPT on August 6, improving its GPT-5.6 Sol tier and — more consequentially — opening GPT-5.6 Luna to free accounts.

The two-model structure is the story. Sol carries the quality flagship position for paying users, while Luna going free turns ChatGPT’s default experience into a wider funnel at a time when Chinese open-weight alternatives are a tap away. Free-tier quality has quietly become a competitive front.

Model-release trackers note the lineup now spans GPT-5.4 through 5.6 in active service — a cadence that makes "which model am I talking to" a moving target even for attentive users, and makes per-tier differentiation part of OpenAI’s pricing strategy.
