DeepSeek makes V4-Pro generally available — and rewrites its price list

DeepSeek’s V4 line moved from preview to production this month: V4-Pro-0813 hit general availability on August 13, with a new price list effective August 16 at 16:00 UTC.

The V4-Flash 0731 build is the quiet standout — same architecture and size as the April preview, fully retrained in post-training, with native Codex support and the Responses API alongside it. DeepSeek’s changelog claims agent capabilities "far exceeding V4-Pro-Preview," and third-party numbers back the direction: 82.7 on Terminal Bench 2.1 and 54.2 on NL2Repo.

Under the hood, V4-Flash pairs 284B total parameters with 13B active and supports up to 384,000-token outputs — a specification aimed squarely at long-horizon coding agents rather than chat. With Claude Code integrations already demoed on the 0731 build, DeepSeek is positioning price-performance as its wedge into the agentic-coding market.
