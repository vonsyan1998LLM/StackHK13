DeepSeek Makes V4-Pro Generally Available — and Rewrites the Price List

V4-Pro is out of preview. The Flash build quietly learned to speak Codex. DeepSeek is betting price-performance is the wedge into agentic coding.

StackHK News Desk·Updated Aug 13, 2026

DeepSeek’s V4-Pro-0813 reached general availability on August 13, with a new price list effective August 16 at 16:00 UTC.

What’s Happening

The V4-Flash 0731 build kept its architecture but was fully retrained in post-training — and added native Codex support alongside the Responses API.

DeepSeek’s changelog claims agent capabilities "far exceeding V4-Pro-Preview." Third-party numbers point the same way: 82.7 on Terminal Bench 2.1, 54.2 on NL2Repo.

Why It Matters

The spec sheet — 284B total parameters, 13B active, up to 384,000-token outputs — targets long-horizon coding agents rather than chat.

Claude Code integrations on the 0731 build are already demoed in the wild.

The target isn’t chat. It’s the agent that ships code.
