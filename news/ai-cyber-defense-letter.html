OpenAI, Anthropic and Google Unite: “The Window to Prepare for AI Cyberattacks Is Closing”

116 companies signed an open letter calling for a global surge in cyber defense — signed by the same industry that spent the year racing model capabilities.

StackHK News Desk·Updated Aug 27, 2026

OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and more than 100 other companies published an open letter on August 27 calling for collective action to strengthen cyber defenses before AI-enabled attacks outpace them.

What’s Happening

The letter — led by OpenAI and signed by ~116 companies including Microsoft — warns of a “limited window” to reinforce defenses, and calls for a global surge in defensive tools and resources.

It references existing frameworks such as the EU’s NIS2 and urges treating cyber defense as an immediate priority across industry and government.

Why It Matters

The same industry spent 2026 racing capabilities; this letter is the first coordinated acknowledgment that the attack surface is growing just as fast.

Security teams get a shared framing — and, more concretely, a signal that AI vendors see defending as their problem too.

The industry that built the new attack surface is asking to help defend it — before the window closes.
