The World’s First AI Agent Phone Debuted at WAIC 2026

A handset that books, buys and coordinates on your behalf. WAIC 2026’s headline act marks the moment agent software left the browser tab.

StackHK News Desk·Updated Jul 20, 2026

The 2026 World AI Conference closed in Shanghai on July 20 after four days of record debuts — headlined by the world’s first commercial AI agent smartphone.

What’s Happening

The agent phone books, buys and coordinates across apps on the user’s behalf — agent software as a pocket default, not a demo.

It shared the floor with MiniMax’s M3 multimodal model, StepFun’s agent operating system and Huawei’s Atlas 950 supernode system.

Why It Matters

State media counted 300+ global product firsts across the four days.

International coverage read the conference as China’s answer to the application-layer question.

Not who has the best model — who puts agents in pockets first.
