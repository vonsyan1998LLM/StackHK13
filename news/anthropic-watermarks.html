Anthropic Starts Watermarking Everything Claude Writes

Every Claude model launched after August 2 embeds machine-readable markers in its output. Detection is becoming a default feature, not an add-on.

StackHK News Desk·Updated Aug 14, 2026

Anthropic confirmed that all Claude models launched on or after August 2, 2026 embed machine-readable watermarks in AI-generated text — and published answers to the questions everyone asked about it.

What’s Happening

The watermark is invisible to readers but machine-detectable — designed to let downstream systems verify whether text came from Claude.

Anthropic published a rare explainer addressing how the marker works, what it survives, and what it doesn’t.

Why It Matters

Provenance is becoming a product feature: watermarks, content credentials and detection APIs are arriving across the industry at the same time.

The open questions — robustness under paraphrase, false positives, who gets detection access — are exactly the ones regulators are now asking.

Provenance is becoming a default feature, not an add-on.
