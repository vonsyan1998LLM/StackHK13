Salesforce and Anthropic announce Claudeforce — Claude lands in the #1 AI CRM

Salesforce and Anthropic announced Claudeforce on August 26 — a deep integration that puts Claude inside the world’s dominant CRM, with an open beta slated for September.

Claudeforce embeds Claude into Salesforce’s sales, service and workflow surfaces, letting reps draft, summarize and act on CRM data through the assistant. For Salesforce it is a statement about which model family it trusts with customer data; for Anthropic it is distribution — the kind established at the enterprise layer where deals are measured in seats, not tokens.

The announcement lands in the middle of a broader enterprise land-grab. Claude has been gaining on enterprise buying criteria built around safety documentation and controllability, and a CRM tie-up converts that reputation into shelf space.
