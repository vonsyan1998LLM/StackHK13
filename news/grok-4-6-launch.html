Grok 4.6 Arrives With a 500K Context Window — and Lands in GitHub Copilot

xAI’s coding-focused update ties GPT-5.6 Sol Max on the AA Intelligence Index, ships into Copilot and Microsoft Foundry — and Grok 5 is still waiting.

StackHK News Desk·Updated Aug 12, 2026

xAI launched Grok 4.6 on August 12: a 500,000-token context window, configurable reasoning levels and a particular focus on long-running coding agent workflows.

What’s Happening

On the AA Intelligence Index, Grok 4.6 scored 61 — tying GPT-5.6 Sol Max and one point below Fable 5 Max.

By August 14 it was available inside GitHub Copilot, and xAI added it to Microsoft Foundry — distribution wins that matter as much as benchmarks.

Why It Matters

Grok 5 remains unreleased despite an early-2026 target — 4.6 is the consolidation release while the next flagship waits.

The strategic play is visible: put the model where developers already work (Copilot, Foundry) and compete on workflow, not hype.

What’s New

Grok 4.6 ships meaningfully better reasoning and coding scores, faster streaming, and deeper X-platform grounding — the two axes xAI has been pulling on all year. Early benchmarks place it firmly in the frontier conversation rather than at the kids’ table.

The Distribution Play

The strategic story remains distribution: Grok is bundled into X’s premium tiers, reaching an audience that will never visit a model comparison site. That install base, plus real-time data access, is a commercial position no benchmark captures — and it is growing.

The Open Questions

Enterprise credibility is the gap: procurement teams want the admin tooling, compliance posture and support commitments that incumbent vendors have spent years building. Watch xAI’s enterprise announcements — that is where the next phase of the race will be decided.

The Key Facts

  • Reasoning and coding gains
  • Deeper X real-time grounding
  • Bundled into X premium tiers
  • Enterprise tooling still the gap
  • Frontier-conversation benchmark placement
