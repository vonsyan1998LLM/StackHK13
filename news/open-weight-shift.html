Open-weight AI’s center of gravity is drifting east, Stanford index shows

Two data points from Stanford’s 2026 AI Index are reshaping how the industry reads the model race: the US–China quality gap has narrowed dramatically, and open-weight downloads now favor Chinese models.

The index counts 50 leading US models against 30 from China — but the trend line matters more than the snapshot. The comprehensive performance gap between the best American and Chinese frontier models has shrunk to the point where sourcing decisions are increasingly about price, licensing and latency rather than capability.

Chinese labs shipped five major open-weight releases in eight weeks this summer, and global developers took notice: for the first time, downloads of Chinese open-weight models exceeded American ones. Silicon Valley teams, as 21�jingji put it, have started quietly switching foundations.
