Detailed Analysis

Where Grammarly Wins

  • More intuitive and user-friendly interface
  • Better AI-powered rewriting and tone detection
  • Wider platform integration (Google Docs, mobile)
  • Generous free tier for basic grammar checking
  • Real-time collaboration features for teams

Where ProWritingAid Wins

  • 20+ detailed writing reports (style, pacing, dialogue)
  • Superior readability and sentence structure analysis
  • Scrivener integration for book writers
  • Lifetime license option saves money long-term
  • More detailed explanations for each suggestion

Our Verdict

Choose Grammarly if you need a polished, easy-to-use writing assistant that works everywhere — emails, docs, social media. Its AI rewriting and tone detection are best-in-class. Choose ProWritingAid if you're a serious writer (fiction or non-fiction) who wants deep style analysis, detailed reports, and doesn't mind a steeper learning curve. The lifetime deal makes it a bargain.
