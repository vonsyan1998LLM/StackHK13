FAQ

Questions, answered

How does StackHK score AI tools?

Every tool is tested hands-on for 2–4 weeks and scored across 5 dimensions: quality of output, ease of use, value for money, speed & reliability, and support & docs. The overall score is a weighted average, and we re-test whenever a major update ships.

How many AI tools has StackHK tested?

We currently cover 57+ tools across 6 categories — writing & chat, coding, productivity, image & video, audio & voice, and business AI — including AI-native products and AI features inside established SaaS platforms.

Are the rankings influenced by vendors?

No. We buy our own subscriptions, run our own prompts and datasets, and publish the tests behind each score. Vendors never see reviews before publication and cannot pay for placement.

Which category should I start with?

Start with the category that matches your daily work: Writing & Chat for communication and research, Coding for building software, Productivity for meetings and automation, Image & Video for creative work, Audio & Voice for voiceovers and music, and Business AI for CRM, analytics and marketing.

How often are category rankings updated?

Scores are reviewed monthly and re-tested after major model or feature releases. Best-of roundups are refreshed whenever a tool enters or leaves the top tier.
